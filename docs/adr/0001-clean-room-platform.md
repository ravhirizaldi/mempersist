# 0001: Clean-room memory platform

- Status: Accepted

## Context

Engram demonstrates the product category, but its BSL restrictions and commercialization goals require an independent implementation.

## Decision

Use only public product/architecture ideas as research. Design original modules, schemas, IDs, algorithms, tests, comments, and repository structure. Never copy or translate Engram source.

## Consequences

Development may take longer, but ownership and future licensing remain clear. Similarities must be explainable by requirements or standard Cloudflare/MCP patterns.

## Alternatives

Forking or adapting Engram was rejected because it violates the clean-room and licensing constraints.
